How to Play

🎯 Goal. Push the ball into the opponent's net. Most goals before the clock runs out wins.

🕹️ Move. Touch and drag anywhere on the right half — a joystick appears under your thumb. Drag to steer; further = faster.

👟 Trap & shoot. Hold the KICK pad and run into the ball — it sticks to your feet for up to half a second so you can line up. Let go to kick or pass in the way you're facing. The longer you hold, the harder the shot (the yellow ring shows the charge).

🧲 Magnet. By default the ball is free once you release. Turn up Ball Magnet in setup for a passive stick that keeps the ball at your feet even without holding KICK.

🏆 Ranked. Single-player matches vs bots earn RP and climb the rank ladder (Wood → Legend); beating harder bots is worth more. Training mode is free practice with no clock. Tied timed games go to sudden-death overtime.

⚡ Dribble. Nudge the ball along with your body as you run, then tap KICK to fire it. Turn up the Ball Magnet in setup (0–100) to make the ball stick to your feet for easier carrying; 0 plays like a free puck.

👥 2-Player. Lay the phone flat between you. Player 1 uses the bottom controls, Player 2 the top (rotated) controls.

🎮 Controllers. Set Input to Controllers and connect gamepads (press a button to wake them). Each pad takes a seat — you first, then an opponent, then a teammate — so 2 pads is 1v1, 8 pads is a full 4v4. Left stick moves, A / RT kicks. Empty seats stay bots.
